Guests holds everyone who has booked or stayed, with their stays and spending.
- **Add guest**, or guests are created when you make a booking.
- Search by name, phone, email or ID number. Open a guest to see stays, **Completed stays** and **Room revenue**, then **Edit**.
- Fields: name, phone, email, **ID type** and **ID / passport number**, nationality, date of birth, address, **Notes / preferences** (e.g. "Prefers ground floor, allergic to nuts") and **VIP guest**.
- Deleting a guest needs a manager.
