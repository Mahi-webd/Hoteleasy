**Set up:** Inventory → "Set up stores and starter items" creates Main store, Kitchen, Bar and Housekeeping stores. Link POS outlets to the store they take stock from.
- **Stock**: every item with quantity per store, reorder level and value. **Low stock** filter, and **Draft purchase order** from low items.
- **Receive goods**: from a purchase order or not, into a store, with the supplier's invoice number; optionally add the supplier's bill to Bills to pay.
- **Transfer / issue**: move stock between stores, or issue to a department. **Record waste** for spoilt or broken items.
- **Purchase orders**: **New order** → **Approve** (manager) → print/send → **Receive**.
- **Counts**: **Start a count**, enter what's on the shelf, **Post count** (manager PIN). Stock is corrected.
- **Recipes** (POS Setup → item → Recipe): ingredients come out of the outlet's store when items are sent to the kitchen or bar. **Costs** shows food cost % and margins.
- **History**: every stock change; nothing can be edited.
