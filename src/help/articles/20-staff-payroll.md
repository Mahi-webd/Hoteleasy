**Staff & rota** (managers):
- **Staff**: everyone who works here, with or without a login. **Add team logins** brings in invited users; add guards, gardeners and casuals without logins. Pay type (monthly, per day, per hour), allowances, KRA PIN, NSSF, SHA numbers, paid by M-Pesa/bank/cash.
- **Rota**: click a day to add a shift; **Copy to next week**; publish so staff see it.
- **Today**: who's on duty and who's late. **Clock in** staff without the app; correct clock times (with a reason).
- **Leave**: approve requests or **Record leave**.
- **Hours & pay**: hours worked, overtime, absent days, pay estimate, CSV for payroll.
- **Payroll**: pick the month → **Refresh from time clock** → check → **Approve** → **Mark paid** (adds wages as an expense and PAYE, NSSF, SHIF, housing levy as bills due on the 9th) → **Payslips** to print. **Tax rates** holds the statutory rates.
**My shifts** (everyone): your rota, **Clock in** / **Clock out**, **Ask for leave**, **My payslips**.
