Close each day once it's over (usually at night, or first thing the next morning).
1. Night audit opens on the next day to close. Fix anything under **checks**: guests who should have arrived (check in or mark **No-show**), guests past check-out, open bills and cash shifts (warnings).
2. Add **Notes for the manager** (e.g. power cut, noisy guests).
3. Press **Close**. That day's payments, charges and restaurant bills are locked; corrections go on the next day.
**Manager's report:** each closed day has a printable daily report: occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, revenue, money taken by method, balances, cash shifts and notes.
**Reopen** (owner or manager): only the latest closed day, with a reason.
**Error "DAY_CLOSED" / day is closed:** you're trying to change something on a closed day; post a correction today instead, or ask a manager to reopen.
