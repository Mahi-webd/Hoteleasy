**Expenses & profit:**
- **Expenses**: add what you spend (date, paid to, category, amount, VAT if you have an eTIMS invoice, paid or unpaid with due date). Tick "Repeats every month" for rent, salaries, internet; copy them into the new month.
- **Bills to pay**: unpaid supplier bills, earliest due first: **Mark paid** with the M-Pesa/bank reference.
- **Profit & loss** by month, with VAT and levy estimates. **Budget** per line. **Categories**.
**Reports**: occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, revenue by department, tax summary, payments by method, where bookings come from, nationalities, unpaid guest bills, for any period. Print, or download CSV (needs the download permission).
**Owner overview**: all your hotels on one page: revenue today, month to date, occupancy, collected, owed, bills due and alerts.
