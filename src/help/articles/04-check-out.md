---
title: Checking a guest out and settling the bill
screen: reservations
tags: check out, departure, settle bill, invoice, leaving, pay balance, company pays, late checkout, early departure
---
1. Reservations → **Departures** (or **In house**) → press **Check out** on the guest.
2. The bill opens. Check the charges with the guest (room nights, restaurant and bar items charged to the room, extras).
3. If there is a balance, take the payment on the **Payments** tab (cash, M-Pesa, card...). The button shows "Take KES … first" until the bill is paid.
4. Press **Check out & issue invoice**. The tax invoice is created and can be printed; the room goes to housekeeping for cleaning.

**Company pays:** if the booking is billed to a company account, the company's share moves to the company's account at check-out, and the guest only pays their extras.

**Guest leaves owing money** (credit agreed by a manager): a manager can use "Check out with … owing". It shows later under Billing → Open bills → "Checked out, still owing".

**Leaving early:** check out on the day they leave; unused future nights are not charged.

**Late check-out:** the bell shows guests past check-out time. Extend the booking, or add a late check-out charge on the bill with **Add to bill**.

**No Check out button?** Your role may not be allowed to check guests out; ask a manager.
