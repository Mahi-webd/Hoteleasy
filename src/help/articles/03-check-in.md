---
title: Checking a guest in
screen: reservations
tags: check in, arrival, give room, key, registration card, id, walk in, room not ready, online check-in
---
1. Reservations → **Arrivals** tab (today's arrivals).
2. Press **Check in** on the guest's row.
3. Choose the **Room**. Only rooms of the booked type that are free for the whole stay are listed. If a room shows as dirty or under maintenance you get a warning: make sure it's ready first.
4. Press **Check in** (green). The room becomes occupied, and the room nights start going onto the guest's bill.

**Before or at check-in:**
- If the guest did **online check-in**, their ID and signed registration card are already there: see Online check-in.
- To print a **registration card** for signing: Online check-in → the guest → **Registration card**.
- Take a deposit or payment from the bill: press **Bill** on the row (see Taking payments).

**Guest arrived early or the room isn't ready:** check them in when the room is ready; Housekeeping shows the room as "Urgent (guest arriving)".

**No Check in button?** Either the arrival date is in the future, or your role can't check guests in (ask a manager).
