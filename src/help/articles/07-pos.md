**Start of shift:** Restaurant & Bar POS → **Open shift**, count your float (opening cash). You can take orders without a shift, but not payments.

**Take an order:**
1. Choose the outlet (Restaurant, Bar, Room service, Pool bar...) at the top.
2. Tap a table, or **Walk-in / takeaway** (name optional), or a checked-in **Guest**.
3. Tap menu items to add them (use the search box). Items with options ask "How cooked?", extras, etc. Add a **Note for the kitchen** if needed.
4. Send the order: items go to the kitchen or bar screen.

**Get paid:** press **Pay**. Choose **Cash** (enter cash received; change is shown), **M-Pesa** (send a prompt, or type the code), **Card**, or **Room** to charge it to a checked-in guest's room bill (search by room number or name and confirm with the guest). **Split evenly** splits between people; pay part with one method and the rest with another.

**Bill menu** (the ⋯ button on the bill):
- **Split items to new bill**, **Move to another table**, **Change number of guests**.
- **Discount** and **Service charge**: need the discount permission, or a manager types their PIN.
- **Void** an item (removes it, shows on the voids report) or **Comp** (free of charge). Food already sent to the kitchen needs a manager's approval.
- **Void whole bill**.
- **Board meal**: for guests on a meal plan, covers their included meal.

**Receipt:** after paying press **Receipt** to print.

**End of shift:** POS → your shift → **Close shift**: count all cash in the drawer (with the float) and enter it. The Z report shows expected cash and over/short. Print it and hand in the cash.

**"Open your cash shift before taking payment"**: open a shift first. **"Send or remove unsent items"**: send the new items to the kitchen first.
