Every confirmed booking has its own online check-in link. The guest fills in their ID or passport, trip details and other guests, reads the house rules and signs on their phone.

**Send the link:** Online check-in → **WhatsApp**, or Reservations → the booking's menu → **Send online check-in (WhatsApp)** or **Copy online check-in link**. Best a day or two before arrival.
**When the guest has filled it in:** Online check-in → **Review**: compare "From the guest" with "On file now", then **Accept details** or **Accept and check in**.
**Registration card:** **Registration card** opens the signed card to print (Kenyan law requires a guest register). Reception checks the ID on arrival.
**House rules** shown on the card: edit them at the bottom of Online check-in (managers).
