**Rooms** (managers): **Room types** first (name, **Rate / night**, max guests, amenities), then **Add room** (number, floor, type). Each room shows live status; **Set status** marks clean/dirty. **Report a problem** sends it to maintenance.

**Rates & seasons:**
- **Rate calendar**: the price of each room type every night. "Booking page prices" shows online prices.
- **Seasons** → **New season**: name, colour, first and last night, which nights of the week (a weekend uplift = Fri and Sat nights), **Change normal rates by (%)** and/or a fixed price per room type, **Minimum stay**, **No arrivals (online)**, and **Priority** (Normal for weekends, Higher for seasons, Highest for holidays, so Christmas beats a weekend inside it). Existing bookings keep their prices.
- **Meal plans & packages** → **New plan**: Bed & breakfast, Half board, Full board or a package with extras; price per person per night; whether it's offered online.
- **Meal counts**: how many breakfasts, lunches and dinners plan guests have each day (for the kitchen).
