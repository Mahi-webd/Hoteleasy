**Report a problem** (anyone): Maintenance → **Report problem**, or from a room on the Rooms screen. Choose the room or place, what's wrong, how urgent, and tick **Room can't be used until fixed** if it can't be sold. Maintenance sees it straight away.
**Fixing it** (maintenance staff and managers): open the issue, set **Status**, **Priority**, **Assigned to**, and write **What was done**. When fixed, an out-of-service room goes to housekeeping for cleaning before it can be sold again.
Tabs: **Open**, **Mine**, **Fixed**, **All**; **Rooms out of service** lists rooms that can't be sold.
