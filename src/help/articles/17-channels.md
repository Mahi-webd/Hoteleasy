Each calendar link connects one room to one listing, so the same night isn't sold twice.
- **Add calendar link** (managers): choose the site and the room, paste the site's calendar export link. Then copy **our** link (copy button) into the site's calendar import. **How to connect** has the steps for Airbnb and Booking.com.
- Bookings from the site arrive within about 10 minutes (or press **Sync now**) as reservations named "Airbnb guest" / "Booking.com guest" with price 0. At check-in, add the real name and the price (the site collects the money).
- If the site cancels, the booking is cancelled here too.
- **Double booked** (red): the room was already taken. The site's booking is kept without a room: move one of the guests to another room, and close the dates on the site.
- Booking.com calendar sync only works when each room is its own room type (one unit).
