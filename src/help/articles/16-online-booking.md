**Booking page:** Online booking & reviews → **Setup**: switch it on, set the page address, headline, about text, photo, WhatsApp number, cancellation days, minimum/maximum nights, payment instructions and house rules. **Rooms offered online** and online prices. Share the link (**Copy booking link**) on Google, Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp, or add the **Book now** button to your website.
**Online bookings** arrive in Reservations marked "Website", without a room: assign one at check-in.
**Guests** can look up, change or cancel their booking with the code and their email or last name, check in online, and pay a deposit by M-Pesa if allowed.
**Reviews:** after check-out, **Ask for feedback** sends each guest their private link. Read scores and comments, **Reply**, and choose which reviews show on the booking page.
**Guest emails:** confirmation and pre-arrival emails (once email sending is set up for HotelEasy).
