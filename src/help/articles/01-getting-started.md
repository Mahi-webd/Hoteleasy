HotelEasy runs the whole hotel: front desk, billing, restaurant and bar, housekeeping, maintenance, stock, staff and reports. Everyone signs in with their own email, and sees only the parts their role allows.

**Moving around.** The menu on the left lists the screens you can use, grouped as Front office, Food & beverage, Management, Operations and Admin. On a phone, tap the menu button at the top left. If something you need is missing from your menu, your role doesn't include it: ask the owner or a manager.

**Several hotels.** If you work for more than one hotel, tap the hotel name at the top left to switch.

**The bell** at the top right shows what needs attention now (late check-outs, guests waiting, requests not answered, the day not closed...). Tap an alert to go to the right screen.

**First setup for a new hotel (owner or manager), in this order:**
1. Settings & Team: hotel details, VAT and tourism levy, invoice settings, then invite your staff.
2. Rooms: add room types (with the normal rate per night), then the rooms.
3. Rates & seasons: weekends, high season, meal plans (optional).
4. Restaurant & Bar POS → Setup: outlets, tables and menu (there is a "Set up with sample menu" button).
5. Inventory (optional): stores and items, recipes for menu items.
6. Staff & rota: add staff, pay rates, shift types.
7. Online booking & reviews: switch on your booking page.

**Signing in.** Staff get an email invitation from Settings & Team and sign up with that same email. "Forgot password" on the sign-in page sends a reset link.
