---
title: Common messages and what to do
screen: dashboard
tags: error, problem, not working, can't, permission, day closed, rate, shift, mpesa code used, no room, sold out, slow, offline
---
- **"You don't have permission" / a button is missing**: your role doesn't allow it. Ask the owner or a manager, or ask them to do it.
- **Day is closed (DAY_CLOSED)**: night audit locked that day. Put the correction on today, or ask a manager to reopen the day.
- **"You can't change the room rate"**: only people with the room-rate permission can override season prices.
- **"Open your cash shift before taking payment"** (POS): POS → Open shift.
- **"Send or remove unsent items before taking payment"** (POS): send the new items first.
- **M-Pesa code already used**: that payment is recorded already; check Payments.
- **No room free / Sold out**: the room type is full for those nights; try other dates or types, or the Calendar tab.
- **Room marked dirty at check-in**: ask housekeeping to finish it (Housekeeping → Start / Done).
- **The page looks out of date**: refresh the browser. Check your internet connection.
