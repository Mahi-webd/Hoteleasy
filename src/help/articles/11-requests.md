Guests scan the QR code in their room and ask for towels, cleaning, a repair, a taxi, a wake-up call and more. Requests appear live in **Guest requests** with a sound, for the right team.
- Press **Send** / **Done** as you handle it, and **Reply** to tell the guest (they see it on their phone). Requests waiting over 15 minutes are highlighted.
- A guest phoned or asked at the desk? **Log a request**.
- Repairs also open a maintenance ticket automatically.
- **Setup & QR codes** (managers): **Print QR codes** for the rooms, choose **What guests can ask for** and which team gets each, and **Good to know** (Wi-Fi password, breakfast hours…) shown to guests.
