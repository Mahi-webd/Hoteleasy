Open a guest's bill from Reservations (**Bill** button) or Billing & Payments → **Open bills**.

**Take a payment or deposit:** Payments tab → choose the method (cash, M-Pesa, card, bank transfer...) → amount → reference → record it.
- M-Pesa: send a pay prompt to the guest's phone (see M-Pesa), or tap "Guest already paid? Type the M-Pesa code instead" and type the 10-character code from their SMS (e.g. TK45ABC1XY). The same code can't be used twice.

**Add a charge** (laundry, minibar, transfer, damage...): Charges → **Add to bill**: item, quantity, price.

**Mistake?** Press **Void** on the charge or payment and give a reason. Front desk can undo their own entry within a few minutes; after that a manager or accountant voids it. Once the night audit has closed a day, that day's entries can't be voided: put a correction on today instead.

**Refund:** Payments → **Refund to guest** (needs the refund permission).

**Interim bill** (before check-out): **Print interim bill**. It says "This is not a tax invoice".

**Bill a company:** **Move to company account** moves the balance to a company's account (see Company accounts).

**Invoices:** created automatically at check-out. Billing & Payments → **Invoices** to find and reprint one.

**Cash-up at the end of a shift:** Billing & Payments → **Payments & cash-up**: totals by method and **By staff member** for any period. Print it to check each cashier's drawer and M-Pesa.
