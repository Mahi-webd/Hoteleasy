When M-Pesa prompts are switched on, you can send a payment request straight to the guest's phone. The guest enters their M-Pesa PIN and the payment records itself with the receipt number.

**On a guest bill:** Bill → Payments → method M-Pesa → enter the amount → the guest's phone number (0712 345 678) → **Send prompt for KES …**. Wait for "Paid … receipt …". Amounts are whole shillings (rounded up).
**At the POS:** Pay → M-Pesa tab → phone → **Send prompt**. The bill closes when paid.
**Online guests** can pay the first night or whole stay from their booking page, if the hotel allows it.

**"The guest cancelled the prompt" / "didn't answer in time":** just send it again. Check the phone is on, has M-Pesa, and enough balance.
**"Stop waiting":** stops watching. If the guest pays anyway, it is still recorded.
**Guest paid to the till or paybill directly:** type the M-Pesa code instead.

**Setting it up (owner or manager):** Settings & Team → **M-Pesa prompts**: choose Test or Live, Paybill or Till, enter the number, the Daraja consumer key, secret and passkey, **Test the keys**, then switch **Use M-Pesa prompts** on. Live keys come from Safaricom after the hotel's Lipa na M-Pesa Online application.
