**Settings & Team** (owner and managers):
- **Hotel details**: name, address, phone, check-in and check-out times, VAT %.
- **Team** → **Invite someone**: their email and role. They sign up with that email and get access. Change a person's role from the list, or remove them.
- **Billing & invoices**: KRA PIN, tourism levy %, invoice prefix and number, M-Pesa paybill/till and bank details shown on invoices, invoice footer.
- **M-Pesa prompts**: see the M-Pesa guide.
**Built-in roles**: Owner (everything), Manager, Front desk, Housekeeping, Restaurant, Accountant, Maintenance, Storekeeper.
**Roles & permissions** (owner only): **New role** makes a custom role (e.g. "Night auditor"): pick a job type, start from a built-in role, and tick exactly what it can view, create, edit and delete, plus special rights (refunds, voids, discounts, close the day, change room rates, download spreadsheets…). Give it to people in Settings & Team. **Who can do this?** shows everyone who has a permission.
If a screen or button is missing for someone, their role doesn't include it.
