For businesses that book now and pay later.
- **Add company**: name, KRA PIN, contact, **Pays within (days)**, **Credit limit**, what they **Usually pay for** (everything or the room only), and **Negotiated rates** (a discount or a fixed rate per room type).
- When booking, choose the **Company account**, **Who pays** and the **Order / LPO number**. At check-out the company's share moves to their account.
- **Payment received** (or **Credit note**, **Extra charge**) records what they pay. **Print statement** for any period. **Ageing (CSV)** shows how long amounts have been owed.
- Over the credit limit, a manager has to approve.
