Rooms appear here automatically when a guest checks out. **Urgent (guest arriving)** shows rooms needed for today's arrivals first.
- **My rooms** shows tasks assigned to you; **Everyone** shows all.
- Tap **Start** when you begin a room and **Done** when it's ready. The room becomes available to sell. **Undo start** if you tapped by mistake.
- Found a problem? **Report a problem** (sends it to maintenance).
- Supervisors: **Add task** (deep clean, extra bed, VIP amenities…) and **Assign to** a housekeeper; **Daily service for occupied rooms** creates the day's stay-over cleaning.
