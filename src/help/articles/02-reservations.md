**New booking:** Reservations → **New reservation** (top right), or click an empty cell on the **Calendar** tab to book that room for that night.
1. **Guest:** type a name or phone to find a returning guest, or choose **Add as new guest** and fill in the name and phone.
2. **Check-in** and **Check-out** dates, **Adults** and **Children**.
3. **Room type**, then a room, or **Assign later** (a room is chosen at check-in).
4. **Meal plan** (Room only, Bed & breakfast, Half board...) if your hotel has plans.
5. The price comes from Rates & seasons automatically. Changing it by hand needs the "change the room rate" permission; otherwise you see "Season price. Ask a manager to change the rate." **Use season prices** puts the automatic price back.
6. **Booked via** (walk-in, phone, website, Booking.com...), **Company account** if a company pays (choose **Who pays** and an **Order / LPO number**), and **Special requests**.
7. Save. The booking gets a code like HE-ABC123.

**Walk-in guest:** make the booking with today as check-in, then press **Check in** on it straight away.

**Change a booking** (dates, room, guest, extend a stay): open it from the list and edit, then save. The price is worked out again for the new dates.

**Finding a booking:** the tabs are **Arrivals**, **In house**, **Departures**, **Upcoming**, **Calendar** and **Search all**. The search box takes a guest name, phone, room or booking code.

**Calendar tab:** bars start mid-day on arrival and end mid-day on departure. Click an empty cell to book that room.

**Can't find a free room?** The room type is full for those nights. Try another room type or dates, or check the Calendar tab.
