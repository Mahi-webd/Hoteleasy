Restaurant & Bar POS → **Setup** (managers):
- **Menu items:** add or edit an item: name, category, price (including tax), **Goes to** kitchen or bar, which outlets sell it, options, VAT. Mark **sold out** for today or hide it. **Recipe** links ingredients for stock and food cost.
- **Categories**, **Options (modifiers)** such as "How cooked?" or "Extras" with extra prices.
- **Outlets & service charge:** each outlet has its own tables, bills and sales report.
- **Tables:** add tables per outlet (room service and takeaway don't need tables).

**Approval PIN:** managers set a 4–6 digit PIN (POS → **Set PIN** or Setup → Your approval PIN). They type it on a waiter's screen to approve voids and discounts.

**Kitchen & bar screen:** open "Kitchen & bar screen" on a tablet in the kitchen. Choose the station (kitchen or bar) and outlet. Tap an order to move it from new → preparing → ready. Use full screen.

**Sales report:** POS → **Sales**: sales by outlet, payment, waiter, top items, tax, and every void, comp and discount with who approved it.
